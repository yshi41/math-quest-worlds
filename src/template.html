How to play

  • Move: arrow keys or W A S D, or the pad on a touch screen. Melee: Z, J or Space (button A). Ranged: X or K (button B), it uses ammo that refills slowly and drops from monsters. Q / E or the swap buttons change weapons.
  • Weapons are numbers. Plain monsters just take the damage. A Number Slime loses your weapon's number each hit and pops on exactly 0. A Factor Beetle only takes hits from a number that divides it, and shrinks by that much.
  • Puzzle rooms: read the sign, then do it in the room: hop the stones that fit, shoot the right targets, push blocks onto plates, step on the right tiles, light the torches in order, hit the dials to the code. Each solved room unlocks its chest.
  • Keys: two puzzle rooms and the arena hold keys. Three keys open the boss door.
  • Bosses are beaten with the math: break the shield on exactly 0, divide the Factor Boss down to 1, feed the Minion Boss only the right minions, or stand on the next number in the Pattern Boss's sequence. Bosses drop special weapons, monsters drop coins, hearts, ammo and gear.
