How to play

  • Explore: walk with the arrow keys, W A S D, or the pad on a touch screen. Walk into signs, people and chests to use them.
  • Puzzles are in the world: hop across stones that add up to the sign's number, open the door that follows the rule, pay the troll the right toll, answer the elder to unlock a key.
  • Battles: a monster tells you its HP once. Pick your moves and keep the running total in your head. Land on exactly 0 for double loot; overshoot and the loot scatters.
  • Beat the boss behind the two-key door to clear a region and open the next one on the map.
  • Outfitter: spend what you earn on useful gear (bigger moves, shields, charms, boots), outfits, a pet, and pet things that help on the road.
